ZenShield Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

ZenShield ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome Extension operates regarding your data.

1. Data Collection and Usage

ZenShield is designed to operate strictly on your local device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII), web history, or usage analytics to external servers.

2. Local Storage

The extension utilizes your browser's local storage solely to save your specific user preferences, such as your custom whitelisted websites, active filter toggles, and local blocking statistics. This data never leaves your device.

3. Third-Party Sharing

Because we do not collect your data, we do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer any user information to outside parties.

4. Permissions Justification

ZenShield requires specific browser permissions (such as declarativeNetRequest and host permissions) exclusively to intercept and block advertising and tracking scripts locally on your machine. We do not use these permissions to monitor or record your browsing behavior.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us via the Chrome Web Store support tab.
